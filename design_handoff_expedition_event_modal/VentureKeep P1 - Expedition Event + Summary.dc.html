Notes · 1e
All four are feedable from existing data: HP state, loot totals so far, days planned vs elapsed, party morale. None needs a new system.
i reads as the game's voice and needs no interaction — safest default. ii is a one-line change but thin. iii is the clearest but adds a hover state that mouse-only players get; must also work on focus. iv is the only one that frames the choice as a wager, which is what the economy actually is.
Recommendation
Ship i + iv together: the banked/at-risk bar above the row, a consequence line under each button. Switch the flow in 1g between all four with the Stakes tweak.
Copy rule
Never promise an outcome. “Melisande is at 1 HP” is honest; “you will probably lose someone” is not.

Notes · 1f
The audit bug is a copy bug: the Summary prints the planned end date as if it happened. Any of these fixes it; they differ in how much they dramatise the retreat.
i is the game's voice and needs no new component. ii is compact but strike-through reads as an error, not a decision. iii is the most literal but spends two lines on a fact the player chose. iv is the loudest and reuses Badge as-is.
Recommendation
i + iv: the badge for scannability in lists, the parenthetical for the truth. Used in 1g step 5.
Rule
A completed expedition never shows a “planned” number at all — the plan is only interesting when it was broken.
